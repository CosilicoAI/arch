Kept the Israeli OpenLaw statutory labels that qualify a table in the provision body rather than lifting them into a positionless metadata list. A parenthesised note inside a table cell (a temporary-order substitution for that cell's value, a repeal date, an expiry marker) and a parenthesised, colon-terminated label printed above a table (which of two otherwise identical tables this one is) now stay where the source prints them, and are recorded in metadata.statutory_notes. Without them NII לוח י׳ printed its two §337(א)/§340(א) contribution-rate tables back to back under identical headers with nothing to tell them apart, and two entries of לוח ח׳2 read as in force with their repeal and expiry markers deleted. Every other law-note is unchanged: amendment history, OpenLaw's indexed-amount glosses, its footnote letters and its comparison lead-in still never reach a body.

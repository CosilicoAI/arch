Student Allowance rates

Weekly payment rates for Student Allowance.

Important information

You can also use a Student Allowance calculator to work out how much you could get.

What is your situation?

How much you get for Student Allowance depends on your situation.

These rates are based on you having a 'M' tax code. If you're on another tax code, you may get less.

These rates may reduce because of:

  • your income
  • your parents' income.

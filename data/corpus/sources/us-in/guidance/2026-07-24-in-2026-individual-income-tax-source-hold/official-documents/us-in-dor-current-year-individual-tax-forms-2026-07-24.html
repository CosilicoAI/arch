Current Year Individual Tax Forms

Prior year tax forms can be found in the Individual and Fiduciary Prior Year Forms webpage. Not seeing the form you need? Check Miscellaneous Forms.

Do not manually change any part of a tax form, including the year, as it could apply present information or payments to a prior year. You must download and use the correct form for the desired filing year. Including information outside the spaces provided on the forms may not be recorded correctly and may delay the return and refund processes. DOR recommends e-filing your taxes for a quicker and more secure experience.

ES-40

Indiana Form ES-40, Estimated Tax Payment Voucher

Did you know that DOR’s online e-services portal, the Indiana Taxpayer Information Management Engine (INTIME), allows individuals to pay estimated taxes or view their payment history, 24/7?

INTIME offers a quick, safe and secure way to submit payments. You will receive a confirmation right away without having to wait for mail to arrive or checks to clear. Avoid penalties by never missing your payment deadline again.

To begin, go to INTIME.dor.in.gov and select the “Get Started” link under “Indiana Tax Information Management Engine: INTIME.” On the INTIME home page, select “Make a payment” in the “Payments” tile. This will take you to the “Payments” screen. Then simply select your payment type and follow the guide on how to enter your information and make your payment.

If you do not wish to pay your estimated taxes by credit card or ACH (e-check), scroll down to complete and mail Form ES-40 along with your check.

Select INTIME.dor.in.gov to make a payment today.
